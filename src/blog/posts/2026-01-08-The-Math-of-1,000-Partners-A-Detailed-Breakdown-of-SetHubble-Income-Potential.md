## Scenario A: The "Plan" Strategy (Fast Capital)

Imagine a scenario where your focus is on B2B sales or recruiting serious agents. Suppose all **1,000 direct partners** purchase the **Shuttle Plan** ($350/year) to unlock business features and the Binary system.

Here is the math:

1.  **Direct Commission:** You earn **40%** from the plan cost.
    *   $350 × 40% = **$140** per person.
    *   1,000 people × $140 = **$140,000** instant income.

2.  **Binary Bonus:**
    *   These sales generate Points (PV) for the binary system. Let's estimate this brings ~25,000 points into your weak leg (assuming you balance them 500 left / 500 right).
    *   **Extra Income:** Approximately **+$2,500** from the binary spillover cycles.

**Total Result:** ~$142,500 in upfront income just for setting up the network.

## Scenario B: The "Turnover" Strategy (Passive Income)

Now, let’s shift focus to **products**. Imagine these 1,000 people are not just buying plans, but are active merchants or agents selling goods (courses, services, physical items) with an average value of **$100**.

Let's assume the product owner set a **30% total affiliate commission** (e.g., 20% to Level 1, 5% to Level 2, 5% to Level 3). Since you are at the top of this structure, let's conservatively assume you earn a **5% override** on this volume.

**The Power of Volume:**
If each of your 1,000 partners sells just **10 items a day** (using their own referral links):
*   **Daily Sales:** 1,000 partners × 10 sales = 10,000 sales/day.
*   **Daily Revenue:** 10,000 × $100 = **$1,000,000** turnover per day.
*   **Your Cut:** 5% of $1,000,000 = **$50,000 per day**.

*Note: This is an ideal "unicorn" scenario. But even if you achieve only 1% of this result, that is still **$500 per day** ($15,000/mo) of completely passive income.*
